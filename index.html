About Craftech Impex

Where Traditional Indian Artistry Meets Global Standards

Craftech Impex is a Moradabad-based manufacturer and exporter of Indian handicrafts — bringing centuries-old metalworking traditions to homes, hotels and boutiques around the world.

Manufacturing

Every piece is shaped, cast and finished in our own workshops by artisans trained in traditional Indian metal craft.

Quality

Each product passes through rigorous multi-stage inspection to ensure it meets international export standards.

Customization

Bulk Orders

Scaled production capacity built for wholesalers, retail chains, hospitality groups and interior design studios.

Worldwide Export

Reliable packaging and logistics for safe, on-time delivery to buyers across every continent.

Manufacturing

From Raw Metal to Finished Export

Every Craftech Impex piece follows the same disciplined six-stage journey through our workshop.

  1. 01

    Design

    Concepts are sketched and refined, blending heritage motifs with contemporary silhouettes for global taste.

  2. 02

    Sampling

    A hand-made prototype is produced and reviewed against the brief before full production begins.

  3. 03

    Production

    Skilled artisans cast, hammer and shape each piece using traditional techniques at scale.

  4. 04

    Quality Check

    Every unit is inspected for finish, weight, structural integrity and export-grade consistency.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Export-grade cushioning and crating protect every piece for its journey across the world.

  6. 06

    Export

    Documentation, logistics and freight are coordinated for reliable, on-time worldwide delivery.
